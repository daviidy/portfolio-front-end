01)Know your destination: your FIN number

Every trip starts with an address. In money, the address is your FIN number: your Financial Independence number. And it starts with one question: what income do you want when you retire?

Not what you live on today. Not the status quo. The income that pays for the life you deserve, for you and the family you support here and back home.

Monthly income I want when I retire (the life I deserve, not just the bills)
+ Monthly support I want to keep sending to family abroad
= Monthly retirement income I want, × 12 = my destination
You may have heard of the "4% rule." It is built to help you survive retirement, not to create wealth. Your FIN number starts from the income you want, not from a rule that tells you how little to live on.

On a call, we work backward from that income to the number that produces it.

02)The road has 3 goals, in this order

The road to your FIN number is not "invest more." It is three jobs your money must do, one after the other.

Goal 1Income protectionIf you cannot work tomorrow, does the income stop? Protect the paycheck first: health, disability, life.
Goal 2Income replacementBuild assets that pay you so one day your paycheck becomes optional.
Goal 3Generational wealthWhat you leave behind, passed on in a way that protects your kids and the tax bill.
Protect yourself and your family. Grow it tax-free. Pass on a legacy.

Skip goal 1 and one accident can erase years of work. Skip goal 3 and the next generation starts from zero, like you did.

03)The 4 principles of prudent investing

Any vehicle out there, from a savings account to real estate, should be judged by the same four questions.

Principle 1LiquidityCan I get to my money when life happens, without penalty?
Principle 2SafetyIs my principal protected from market loss?
Principle 3Predictable rate of returnCan I plan around what it earns, or is it a guess?
Principle 4Tax-favoredHow much of the growth do I actually keep?

Score the vehicles you use today. Be honest. Few pass all four, which is exactly the point.

04)Savings is the fuel

Picture the road to your FIN number. The vehicle is the car. Savings is the fuel. The best car in the world goes nowhere on an empty tank, and a small tank filled every month beats a big one you never fill.

  1. Name the number. Decide what you will save each month, as a fixed amount, not "whatever is left."
  2. Automate it. Move it the day your paycheck lands, before it has a chance to be spent.
  3. Aim it at the road. Point every saved dollar at a vehicle that passes the four principles.
  4. Raise it. Each raise, add to the amount before your lifestyle absorbs it.
