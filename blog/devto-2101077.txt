<h2>Prerequisites</h2>
<ul>
<li>A Laravel application</li>
<li>A Google reCAPTCHA v2 site key and secret key. Here is how to get it quick:</li>

<p>You will see the site and secret keys. Or you can access it in your console at <a href="https://console.cloud.google.com/security/recaptcha">https://console.cloud.google.com/security/recaptcha</a></p>
