<span data-line=""><span style="--shiki-dark:#E1E4E8;--shiki-light:#24292E">  expect(</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#79B8FF;--shiki-light:#005CC5">Event</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#E1E4E8;--shiki-light:#24292E">.</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#F97583;--shiki-light:#D73A49">new</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#E1E4E8;--shiki-light:#24292E">(</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#79B8FF;--shiki-light:#005CC5">end_time:</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#79B8FF;--shiki-light:#005CC5"> Time</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#E1E4E8;--shiki-light:#24292E">.now).start_time).to eq(</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#79B8FF;--shiki-light:#005CC5">Time</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#E1E4E8;--shiki-light:#24292E">.now)</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="--shiki-dark:#F97583;--shiki-light:#D73A49">end</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>This test fails. Why? You got it, it's because there are two different moments in time.
One way to fix it is to use variables. Like this:</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="--shiki-dark:#e1e4e8;--shiki-light:#24292e;--shiki-dark-bg:#24292e;--shiki-light-bg:#fff" tabindex="0" data-language="ruby" data-theme="github-dark github-light"><code data-language="ruby" data-theme="github-dark github-light" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="--shiki-dark:#E1E4E8;--shiki-light:#24292E">it </span><span style="--shiki-dark:#9ECBFF;--shiki-light:#032F62">'compares times correctly'</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#F97583;--shiki-light:#D73A49"> do</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="--shiki-dark:#E1E4E8;--shiki-light:#24292E">  current_time </span><span style="--shiki-dark:#F97583;--shiki-light:#D73A49">=</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#79B8FF;--shiki-light:#005CC5"> Time</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#E1E4E8;--shiki-light:#24292E">.now</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="--shiki-dark:#E1E4E8;--shiki-light:#24292E">  expect(</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#79B8FF;--shiki-light:#005CC5">Event</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#E1E4E8;--shiki-light:#24292E">.</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#F97583;--shiki-light:#D73A49">new</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#E1E4E8;--shiki-light:#24292E">(</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#79B8FF;--shiki-light:#005CC5">end_time:</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#E1E4E8;--shiki-light:#24292E"> current_time).start_time).to eq(current_time)</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="--shiki-dark:#F97583;--shiki-light:#D73A49">end</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>But let's dig deeper. Let's say we have this:</p>
<p>When we debug:</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="--shiki-dark:#e1e4e8;--shiki-light:#24292e;--shiki-dark-bg:#24292e;--shiki-light-bg:#fff" tabindex="0" data-language="ruby" data-theme="github-dark github-light"><code data-language="ruby" data-theme="github-dark github-light" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="--shiki-dark:#E1E4E8;--shiki-light:#24292E">current_time </span><span style="--shiki-dark:#F97583;--shiki-light:#D73A49">=</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#79B8FF;--shiki-light:#005CC5"> Time</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#E1E4E8;--shiki-light:#24292E">.now</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="--shiki-dark:#79B8FF;--shiki-light:#005CC5">print</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#E1E4E8;--shiki-light:#24292E"> current_time                    </span><span style="--shiki-dark:#6A737D;--shiki-light:#6A737D"># => 2024-11-28 12:06:25 -0600</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="--shiki-dark:#79B8FF;--shiki-light:#005CC5">print</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#79B8FF;--shiki-light:#005CC5"> Event</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#E1E4E8;--shiki-light:#24292E">.</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#F97583;--shiki-light:#D73A49">new</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#E1E4E8;--shiki-light:#24292E">(</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#79B8FF;--shiki-light:#005CC5">end_time:</span><span style="--shiki-dark:#E1E4E8;--shiki-light:#24292E"> current_time).start_time  </span><span style="--shiki-dark:#6A737D;--shiki-light:#6A737D"># => 2024-11-28 18:06:25 UTC</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>This spec passes even though both time are different. Why?
Here is why: when RSpec compares times with eq, it compares the <em>actual</em> moments in time, not their string representations or timezone formats.
So <span data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><code data-language="plaintext" data-theme="github-dark github-light" style="--shiki-dark:#e1e4e8;--shiki-light:#24292e;--shiki-dark-bg:#24292e;--shiki-light-bg:#fff"><span data-line=""><span>2024-11-28 12:06:25 -0600</span></span></code></span>and <span data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><code data-language="plaintext" data-theme="github-dark github-light" style="--shiki-dark:#e1e4e8;--shiki-light:#24292e;--shiki-dark-bg:#24292e;--shiki-light-bg:#fff"><span data-line=""><span>2024-11-28 18:06:25 UTC</span></span></code></span> will be equal.</p>
<h2>The Ultimate Lesson</h2>
<p>When working with time in Rails:</p>
<ol>
<li>Remember ActiveRecord converts to UTC automatically</li>
<li>Store time instances in variables for comparisons</li>
<li>Times are equal if they represent the same moment, regardless of timezone</li>
</ol>
